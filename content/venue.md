The conference is held at the [Crested Butte Center for the Arts](https://crestedbuttearts.org/plan/directions/).

The opening session and the lightning talks/hackathon day presentations are in the King Community Room (top floor, north end of the Center for the Arts).

The Monday night banquet is at the [Crested Butte Museum](https://crestedbuttemuseum.com/visit-us/).
